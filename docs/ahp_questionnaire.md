## How to answer

For each pair, decide which criterion matters more for siting a **decentralised sewage treatment plant in Bhopal**, and how much more, on Saaty's scale:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Equally important |
| 3 | Moderately more important |
| 5 | Strongly more important |
| 7 | Very strongly more important |
| 9 | Extremely more important |
| 2, 4, 6, 8 | In between |

Judge importance **as the criteria actually vary in Bhopal**, not in general. For example, slope scores 9–10 on 96% of the city, so it hardly separates one site from another.

## The criteria

| Criterion | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `residential` | Distance from residential areas (farther = better: odour, health, public acceptance) |
| `water` | Distance from lakes and water bodies (farther = better: protect Upper Lake / Bhojtal) |
| `drainage` | Closeness to natural drainage (closer = better: outfall for treated effluent) |
| `slope` | Gentle slope (flatter = better: construction cost) |
| `roads` | Closeness to roads (closer = better: construction and desludging-truck access) |
| `lulc` | Land cover (barren best: no clearing, lower acquisition conflict) |
| `dem` | Low elevation (lower = better: gravity flow from the catchment) |
