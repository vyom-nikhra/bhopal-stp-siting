# Site check (final model)

Generated by `python -m stp.cli review` for the 66 sites of the final model: AHP weights, 100 m residential cap, 0.56 ha minimum, and hard constraints from 12 months of Dynamic World (no cropland, trees, built or water; 100 m around settlements; a water-frequency flood proxy), keeping the best ~275 ha of eligible land.

The earlier 44-site sheet and labels (before the constraints) are in `archive/`.

- `site_check.csv`: one row per site, pre-filled with automated evidence and Claude's visual labels, plus blank columns for your verification.
- `sites.kml`: the site outlines. Open in Google Earth Pro (File > Open), or import it as a KML file in Google Earth web's Projects panel.
- `claude_visual_labels.csv`: the source of the `claude_*` columns, kept separate so it can be corrected and the sheet regenerated.

## Columns

**Context:** `rank`, `area_ha`, `robust` / `persistence` (share of ±20% weight draws in which the site is still selected), `in_original_46` (overlaps a site from the original QGIS result), `suit_mean`, `drivable_last_mile_m`, `lat`, `lon`, map links.

**Independent products** (`python -m stp.cli crosscheck`), each as "dominant class, share of the site":
- `worldcover_2021`: ESA WorldCover 10 m, 2021
- `esri_io_2023`: Esri / Impact Observatory 10 m land cover, 2023
- `dynamic_world`: Google Dynamic World for the same Sentinel-2 scene (2025-06-09)
- `product_verdict`: majority of the three: `likely_farmland`, `likely_built`, `likely_water`, `likely_trees`, `consistent_open_land`, or `mixed`
- `osm_landuse`: largest OSM `landuse` overlaps (`python -m stp.cli crosscheck-osm`, from a local OSM extract). "none" means no mapped land use touches the site, which is true of all 66: OSM maps land use over only 15% of Bhopal, mostly residential.

**Claude's visual labels** (`claude_cover`, `claude_usable`, `claude_confidence`, `claude_notes`): from true- and false-colour Sentinel-2 chips at 10 m (`outputs/final/chips/`), not high-resolution imagery. Coarse: confidence is `low` except where a site is clearly inside a settlement.

**Your columns** (blank; the regenerate command refuses to overwrite them once filled):

| Column | Values |
|---|---|
| `vyom_agree` | `yes` if Claude's label is right, `no` otherwise |
| `vyom_cover` | if `no`: `bare`, `farmland`, `scrub`, `built`, `water`, `other` |
| `vyom_usable` | if different from Claude's: `yes`, `no`, `unsure` |
| `vyom_notes` | anything notable, e.g. imagery date, brick kiln, new construction |

## Quick route

Check first: `claude_usable = no` (S35, S43: a regular-rows facility, worth identifying), the sites flagged for a high-resolution look (S14, S18, S66), the riverside ones (S48, S50, S53), and the large sites (S17, S38, S41, S53).

Note: `product_verdict` uses the *single* June Dynamic World scene, the least reliable of the three products for telling fallow from open land, while the model itself uses 12 months of Dynamic World. WorldCover (62/66 open land) and Esri (35/66) are the independent checks.
